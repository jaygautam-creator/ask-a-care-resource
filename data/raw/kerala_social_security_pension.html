🏦 Social Security Pension

Social Security Pension Scheme in Kerala

Social Security Pension in Kerala is a monthly financial assistance provided to eligible persons through Local Self-Government Institutions. Currently, the rate of the major social security pensions is ₹2,000 per month.

📋 Major Pension Categories

Sl. No.Pension CategoryGeneral Category of Eligibility
1Old Age PensionEligible senior citizens
2Widow PensionWidows / Those fulfilling specified conditions
3Unmarried Women Pension for Women Above 50 Years of AgeUnmarried women above 50 years of age
4Disability PensionEligible persons with physical / mental disabilities
5Agricultural Labour PensionEligible agricultural labourers
